## What I Build

### 🪦 The Postmortems Series (Primary Work)

In-depth, unsparing autopsies of real-world indie products that failed. Every case study includes exact Git commit counts, traffic metrics, operational costs, financial loss, and root-cause postmortems.

- **Goal**: Help developers avoid multi-month engineering rabbit holes before product-market fit.
- **Latest cases**: [MailIELTS (#001)](/postmortems/mailielts.html) (714 commits, 21 months, $0 revenue), [NanoPhotoStudio (#002)](/postmortems/nanophoto.html) (1,648 commits, 282 PRs, 27 weekly visitors, $0 revenue).

### ⛏️ Utility & Niche Search Tools

Lightweight, rapid-deployment single-page utilities capturing underserved search demand and practical workflows:

- **[The Sift Guide](https://thesiftguide.com)**: Interactive dimension map, portal calculator, and mob index for Minecraft 'The Sift'.
- **IARC Pre-Check**: Pre-submission compliance checker for mobile game developers navigating global age ratings.

### 📈 Systematic Quantitative Trading

Long-term algorithmic trend-following strategies (Turtle-based rules) running continuously on crypto spot and cross-margin markets since 2015.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Who is Louis Cai?

Louis Cai is a 17-year software architect turned indie builder based in China. He openly publishes engineering retrospectives, live code experiments, and postmortem audits of abandoned side projects.

### What is the "Postmortems" series about?

It is a public postmortem archive documenting failed indie software projects with raw, verified numbers—exact commit logs, hosting costs, months spent, and why the product missed the market.

### Are the tools and guides free to use?

Yes. Guides like _The Sift Guide_ and the postmortem articles are 100% free and open to the public without paywalls or subscriptions.

### Can I share or reference the postmortem data?

Yes, all postmortem data and articles can be quoted with attribution back to `louis-cai.github.io`.
