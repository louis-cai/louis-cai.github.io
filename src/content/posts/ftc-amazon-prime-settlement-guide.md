If you recently received an unexpected notification about a **$51.00 deposit** from the *"FTC Prime Subscription Settlement Fund,"* you are part of Phase 3 of the historic **$2.5 Billion settlement** reached between the Federal Trade Commission and Amazon over deceptive enrollment and cancellation flows.

To help consumers safely verify notices without falling prey to active phishing campaigns, here is the breakdown of what is legitimate, what deadlines apply, and how to check your account.

### The 3-Point Legitimacy Whitelist

Before clicking links in any refund notice, verify these three non-negotiable criteria:

1. **Sender Name**: Genuine payments come strictly from `FTC Prime Subscription Settlement Fund`.
2. **Official Web Portals**: Official links point only to `subscriptionmembershipsettlement.com` or `veritaconnect.com`.
3. **Reference Amount**: Standard Phase 3 disbursements are strictly `$51.00`.

*Crucial safety rule*: Neither the FTC nor Amazon will ever ask you to pay a processing fee, courier charge, or provide banking passwords to release your refund.

### Distribution Phases & Deadlines

- **Phase 1 (Completed)**: Automatic deposits ($51) to consumers who used 3 or fewer benefits in 12 months.
- **Phase 2 (Closed)**: Claim-based relief for users who attempted cancellation but failed (claims closed July 27, 2026).
- **Phase 3 (ACTIVE NOW)**: Running from **October 1, 2026 to March 5, 2027**. You must accept your digital payment before **March 5, 2027**, or funds will be voided.
- **Phase 4 (April 2027)**: Supplemental pro-rata top-up (up to $200 total) if uncashed funds remain under the settlement threshold.
