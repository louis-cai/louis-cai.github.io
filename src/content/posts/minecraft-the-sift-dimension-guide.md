During **Minecraft Live 2026**, Mojang Studios officially confirmed **The Sift**—Minecraft's first official new dimension in over 14 years, joining the Overworld, the Nether, and The End.

For an interactive breakdown, biome guides, and launch countdowns, check out the dedicated wiki at **[The Sift Minecraft Guide (thesiftguide.com)](https://thesiftguide.com)**.

---

### What is The Sift?

Mojang describes The Sift as a realm that is *"vibrant, mysterious, and teeming with souls."* Unlike community mods like The Aether, The Sift is completely built by Mojang Studios as native vanilla survival endgame content.

### Confirmed Biomes

1. **The Meadow**: High-contrast, saturated landscape with reactive bioluminescent vegetation and soul pools. Native habitat of the new amphibious frog-like mob.
2. **The Carapace**: A desert wasteland featuring massive fossilized skeletal arches and soul sand terrain.

Full map visuals and mob stats are cataloged at **[thesiftguide.com](https://thesiftguide.com)**.

### Release Roadmap

- **Early Playable Access**: September 29, 2026 via *Minecraft Dungeons II* dimensional rifts.
- **Vanilla Survival Debut**: Slated for 2027 on Java and Bedrock editions.
